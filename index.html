    How many seats can I choose?+

    Choose up to four seats per booking. Click a selected seat again to remove it, or use Clear to start over. Each seat is ৳550 in AC Business class.

    How do the offers work?+

    Select exactly four seats, then apply NEW15 for 15% off or Couple 20 for 20% off. Only one offer applies per booking. If you remove a seat, the offer is removed too.

    Is this a real bus reservation?+

    This is an interactive portfolio demo. No payment is taken, no confirmation email is sent, and no actual travel ticket is issued. The route and schedule are sample information; campaign images are AI-generated illustrations.

    Where can I find my demo tickets?+

    Open My tickets in the navigation. Tickets are saved only in this browser on this device, when browser storage is available. You can download a text copy or remove a demo ticket to release its seats here. Your phone number and email are not stored.
