# Campaign imagery

Both campaign photographs were created for this redesign with the built-in image-generation tool on 3 October 2026. They are illustrative scenes inspired by Sylhet, Bangladesh, not documentary photographs, actual operator vehicles, or promised views on the sample overnight journey. The page alt text and booking information identify this use.

## Hero — journey-768.webp / journey-1536.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: cinematic hero photograph for NEXUS, a premium Bangladesh intercity coach travel website. Create a stunning realistic editorial travel photograph of the lush rolling tea country around Sylhet, Bangladesh, a winding narrow smooth two-lane asphalt road gracefully curving in an S through textured emerald tea fields and dark rainforest trees, distant blue-green misty hills under soft hazy warm morning light. A single elegant unbranded cream-white modern touring coach with deep navy lower panels is traveling on the LEFT lane of the road, seen in a beautiful slightly elevated three-quarter rear view, small to medium in the lower middle of the image. Coach physically realistic: two axles, correct wheels, clean glass, no logos or lettering. The place is the star; no huge mountains or exaggerated fantasy. Rich natural green, warm luminous light, film-like fine grain, sophisticated magazine photography, real vegetation, restrained saturation, medium-format lens. Composition: wide 3:2 landscape; road and coach occupy the center and right-middle so both wide and portrait cropping work. Foreground tea hedges lower left, hills at upper half, keep 15% sky. No text, no UI, no border, no collage, no watermarks, no people or other traffic.

## Destination — sylhet-768.webp / sylhet-1536.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial destination photograph for a Bangladesh bus travel website. A peaceful realistic wide landscape photograph inspired by the clear blue-green rivers and subtropical hills of Sylhet, Bangladesh. Eye-level from pale smooth river stones on the shore, a single slender traditional wooden boat moored at the edge of crystalline teal water on the lower left, lush dark green low hills receding into soft humid distance, a few natural clouds in pale sky. Clean natural scene, no garbage, no buildings, no people, no mountains with snow. Beautiful tactile river stones in foreground, water ripples and reflections. Quiet magazine travel photography, analog film character, natural midday light, restrained colors, no overprocessed HDR. Wide 3:2 composition with horizon in upper third, boat small, generous open water center-right. No text, logos, borders, illustrations or watermarks.

## Delivery

The generated originals were converted to 768 px and 1536 px WebP variants for responsive delivery. The hero loads eagerly; the destination image loads lazily. All files are served locally with the website, without external image services.

Original image files from the previous version remain in this repository. The redesign uses the new campaign images and a small code-native SVG brand mark.
